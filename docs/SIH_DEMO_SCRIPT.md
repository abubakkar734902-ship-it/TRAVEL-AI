# 5-Minute SIH Demo Script

## 0:00 – Problem
Tourism data is fragmented. Travellers need separate apps for planning, weather, maps, reviews, bookings and safety.

## 0:40 – Solution
TravelAI is an AI Travel Copilot that continuously optimizes the journey instead of generating one static itinerary.

## 1:10 – Personalized plan
Enter:
- Destination: Mysuru
- Days: 4
- Budget: ₹15,000
- Interests: Heritage + Food

Generate the itinerary.

## 2:00 – Live intelligence
Refresh Weather + Risk. Explain how the risk engine converts raw signals into a decision.

## 2:30 – Dynamic replanning
Click "Simulate Rain → Replan". Explain:
Weather event → risk engine → AI orchestrator → itinerary replacement.

## 3:15 – Budget intelligence
Add expenses and show remaining budget. Explain that production version predicts overspending and recommends cheaper alternatives.

## 3:45 – Safety
Show SOS, nearby emergency services and safe-route concepts.

## 4:10 – Local economy
Show local marketplace: guides, homestays, food walks, artisan experiences.

## 4:30 – Future/scale
Explain AR recognition, voice assistant, multilingual support, crowd prediction, offline mode and digital travel wallet.

## 5:00 – USP
"TravelAI does not just plan a trip. It continuously understands, predicts, optimizes and adapts the journey in real time."
